## ⚠️ Red Flags & Warnings
- **Timezone Constraint (Major)**: The role specifies that the candidate's main timezone must be **within +/- 2 hours of CET**. Brazil (GMT-3) is 4 hours behind CET (winter) and 5 hours behind CEST (summer). This makes team synchronization difficult unless they offer extreme flexibility.
- **Geographic Hiring Restriction**: The listing title specifies "in Germany". While it is 100% Remote, many European companies require residency/work permits in Germany or the EU to hire, even on a remote basis.

3. **Core Stack Alignment**: Perfect match for Wagner's React, Node.js, and TypeScript skills, combined with his Kubernetes and containerization experience.

## 👎 Cons

2. **No Salary Range Disclosed**: Although they claim financial transparency, the salary range is not disclosed on the job post.

## 🎯 Recommendation
**Status**: **Warning / Low Priority**

Despite having a strong tech stack alignment (React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Kubernetes) and a highly attractive culture (32-hour workweek, AI-friendly workflow), the **timezone restriction of +/- 2 hours CET** and the "in Germany" requirement make it highly likely they will filter out applicants based in South America.

If Wagner is comfortable working early morning hours (e.g., starting at 5:00 AM or 6:00 AM Brazil time) and the company is open to global contractors (B2B), it may still be worth applying. However, we should clarify their geographic hiring policy first.
