## 2. Writing Directives & Style Rules

*Feel free to edit these rules or add new ones below.*

- **Rule 1 (US Professional Tone)**: Maintain a highly professional, confident, and direct US-style tone. Avoid overly formal or subservient phrasing like *"I humbly apply..."* or *"I am writing to express my interest..."*. Instead, start with a direct hook about their business/mission and how you can accelerate it.
- **Rule 2 (Custom Hook)**: Tailor the opening paragraph specifically to the company's product. If they are in fintech, relate to transaction processing. If they are in AI, relate to AI log analysis.
- **Rule 3 (No Clichés)**: Never use generic phrases like *"I am a team player who works hard"*. Every claim must be backed by a brief, punchy achievement from your career.
- **Rule 4 (Formatting)**: Keep paragraphs short (maximum 4-5 lines) to ensure high readability. The letter should easily fit on a single page.
- **Rule 5 (Contact Placeholders)**: Dynamically include your active contact details (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, Florianópolis, Brazil) in the header.
